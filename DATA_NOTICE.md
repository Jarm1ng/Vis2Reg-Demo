# Demonstration assets

The public demonstration includes the prepared **Patient 04 recorded sequence only**:

- `data/frames/f_000000.jpg` through `data/frames/f_000510.jpg`: browser-encoded laparoscopic images.
- `data/frames.json`: image indices, camera parameters and saved manual registration poses.
- `data/meshes.json`: the prepared liver and illustrative internal anatomy meshes.
- `data/datasets.json`: the one-case public manifest.

The four LLR-LUS cases in the local research workspace are not included in this repository or hosted site.

These data are provided as part of the project demonstration. The Three.js MIT license applies to the third-party rendering library, **not** to these images, models or registration records. This repository does not grant an additional general reuse or redistribution license for the demonstration assets. Contact the project maintainers for reuse questions.

The prepared sequence uses saved manual registration. Internal anatomy positions are illustrative and have not been validated as camera-view ground truth. The browser viewer is for research visualization and does not establish clinical accuracy or perform live registration inference.
